Questions, answered

Do I need to install any software or plugins?

No. It’s a self-contained HTML file that runs in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. No installation and no browser extensions to break.

How do I import leads from Google Maps?

Highlight and copy a block of search results from Google Maps, paste it into the “Paste Google Maps Text” box in the app, and click Parse & Add Businesses. Everything is formatted into a clean directory.

Is there a monthly subscription fee?

No. It’s a one-time purchase: $24.99 for Standard or $39.99 for Secured Pro. You own the file for life with unlimited expansions and usage.

Can I use it for different businesses or niches?

Yes. Save multiple copies of the file on your desktop: one for roofing leads, one for real estate, one for local dental practices, and so on.

How does the passcode work in Secured Pro?

You set a master passcode the first time you open the app. It’s stored locally as a SHA-256 hash, and the “Lock App” button hides your data in one click.

What if I forget my passcode?

Secured Pro emails you a 6-digit recovery code that resets your passcode without wiping your lead data.

Can I customize the app myself?

Yes. Open the Settings section inside the app to customize your download to fit your business. In Secured Pro you can also upload your own logo there.

How do I get support?

Email info@aldendatasolutions.com with any questions about your download.

Can I add my own branding?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG logo in Settings and it appears in the header and on the unlock screen.
